Our approach.

How we invest
  1. 01 · Identify

    Thesis-driven discovery

    We build conviction around macro themes like AI, defense, longevity and space, then find the best companies before the world catches on.

  2. 02 · Evaluate

    Rigorous due diligence

    Team quality, market size, competitive moat, unit economics and alignment of incentives.

  3. 03 · Commit

    Patient capital

    When we find something exceptional, we commit, and hold through volatility, noise and shorter time horizons.
